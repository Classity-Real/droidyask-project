# DroidYASS - DroidYetAnotherStockSpace (A DroidSpace Compatibilty)
The YASK Configured Kernel Focused on Droidspace & Root. This contains KernelSU-Next or
APatch (If you want to switch using Direct Installation and choosing "android13") 
# Supported Devices
- Topaz: Android 13 - 16
- Tapas: Android 13 - 16
- Sapphire: Android 13 - 16
- Sapphiren: Android 13 - 16
- Creek: Android 13 - 16
- Xun: Android 13 - 16
# Installation
Requirements:
 - Compatible Device
 - TWRP Installed
 - PC (Or mobile if you have TWRP installed already)
 - Brain
 - ADB (If on PC)
1. Download the AnyKernel.zip on Releases on the right bar
2. Go to TWRP by turning off your device, hold vol up and power and once the logo appears
release the power button and keep the vol up until you see TWRP
3. Go to Advanced > Sideload > Swipe to proceed and you are entered of the Sideload (In mobile go to install and go to the downloaded folder and choose anykernel.zip and go to step 5)
4. Go to your PC and click Win + R and type cmd then enter, then type adb sideload file (replace file by the downloaded any kernel.zip)
5. Wait for it and choose CLO (or GKI, Your choice)
6. Choose KSU Only! You are forced to do so
7. Wait for it and your done! Reboot to finish everything
8. Install ReSUISKU And your done!
